You’re invited to a private conversation

A friend wants to share messages with you that only the two of you can read: ordinary-looking comments and chats, with a secret message behind them.

  1. 1Click Join and save your invitation file – it holds the workspace and password your friend chose.
  2. 2Add the free Secret Message extension to Chrome.
  3. 3Click its icon, open Settings and load the saved file. That’s it – welcome aboard!

The invitation never leaves your browser: it is in the part of the link after #, which is not sent to any server. Keep the file private.
